📰 What do you do in your free time?

Nodir, 14: I'm crazy about board games. Every Thursday I go to a chess club at our school. I'm not very good at chess yet, but I'm getting better. I also collect old stamps – I've got about 500!

Zarina, 15: I'm interested in art, so I draw and paint in my free time. At weekends I go to a painting class. I don't like computer games – I think they're boring.

Timur, 13: My hobby is camping. In summer my dad and I go camping in the mountains. We sleep in a tent and cook on a fire. In the evenings I hang out with my friends in the park and we play football.

Comprehension

Listen or read, then choose the correct answer.

  1. What does Nodir collect?

  2. When does Nodir go to the chess club?

  3. What does Zarina think about computer games?

  4. Where does Timur go camping?

  5. Who goes to a painting class?
