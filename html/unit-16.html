Unit 16 · Grammar

Present simple and present continuous to talk about the future

☆☆☆⚡ 0 XP 🖨 Worksheet🎮 Class quiz

📖 Lesson

Present continuous for arrangements

  • Use it for fixed plans with a time and place (often with other people): I'm meeting Aziz at 6 tomorrow.
  • What are you doing this weekend? — We're visiting my grandparents.
  • Aniq kelishilgan, rejalashtirilgan uchrashuv va tadbirlar uchun.

Present simple for timetables

  • Use it for timetables and schedules (trains, buses, films, lessons, shops): The train leaves at 7:30. The film starts at 8.
  • When does the plane land? · The shop doesn't close until 10 pm.
  • Jadval bo'yicha bo'ladigan narsalar (poyezd, dars, kino) uchun present simple.

Compare

  • Arrangement: I'm flying to Istanbul on Friday (I've got a ticket).
  • Timetable: My flight leaves at 6 am.
  • Plan / intention: I'm going to learn Turkish. · Instant decision: OK, I'll come with you.

📰 Akmal's busy week

Next week is going to be very busy for Akmal. On Monday he's taking his English exam. It starts at 9 o'clock, so he's getting up early. On Tuesday afternoon he's meeting his friends at the new shopping centre.

On Wednesday his cousin is arriving from Moscow. Her plane lands at 6:15 in the evening, and Akmal and his dad are collecting her from the airport. On Thursday he's playing in a basketball match.

On Friday evening the family is going to the theatre. The play begins at 7:00 and finishes at 9:30. 'I'm not doing anything on Saturday,' Akmal says. 'I'm staying in bed all day!'
