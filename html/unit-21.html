📰 Navruz in our mahalla

Navruz is the most important festival in our mahalla. We celebrate it on 21st March, when spring begins. A few days before, everybody cleans their houses and decorates the streets with flowers.

The night before Navruz, the women cook sumalak in a huge pot. It takes all night, and they sing traditional songs while they are cooking.

On the day of Navruz, people wear national costume and visit their neighbours and relatives. Guests are always given tea and sweets. In the centre of the town there is a big parade with music and dancing. For me, the best part is the feeling that everybody is together and happy.
